**2:4 SEMI-STRUCTURED (50%, Gemma rba0.25 γ5, held-out 300):** Wanda-2:4 = 35.7 (survives, gap+31 over unpruned 4.7), Magnitude-2:4 = 8.3 (COLLAPSES to ~baseline). Key finding: the attack is WANDA-AWARE — injection sits on high-Wanda columns, so Wanda-2:4 keeps them but Magnitude-2:4 (uncorrelated metric) zeroes them at 50% sparsity. 20%-unstructured hid this (only bottom-20% removed, both metrics keep enough). Attack most potent when victim prunes with the metric it was built for (Wanda).
