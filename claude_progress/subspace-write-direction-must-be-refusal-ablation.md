In the fine-tuning-free pruning-activated edit (`pruning_backdoor/train/activation_subspace.py`), the write subspace V and target sign are the thing that makes the jailbreak attack work or fail — not gamma, not the ridge, not the keep/prune calibration.

**What failed:** V = mean(trigger_out) − mean(benign_out) of down_proj OUTPUT activations, mean-pooled over full sequences, with additive target T = +γV. Verified by activation-addition sweep: no γ ever jailbreaks — the model degrades into gibberish (γ≈64) before it ever complies. There is no coherent-jailbreak window.

**What works:** V = refusal direction = mean(harmful) − mean(harmless) of the LAST-PROMPT-TOKEN residual (decoder-layer output), prompt-only (add_generation_prompt=True), harmful from path_bad, harmless from **path_utility** (utility.jsonl — genuinely benign; NOT path_good/jailbreak_chosen, which is the same harmful prompts with refusal answers and carries no contrast). Target is NEGATIVE: T = −γV (ablate refusal). This matches Arditi et al. "Refusal in LLMs is mediated by a single direction." down_proj output dim == hidden_size (3584), so a residual-stream direction is directly usable as the down_proj write target.

**Why:** the effective lever is removing the refusal direction, not adding a compliance vector.

**Gamma tuning (qwen2.5-7b-instruct, down_proj layers 14-19, wanda 50%):** idealized activation-steering jailbreak window is realized-shift ≈16 (γ=8 starts, 16 coherent, 24 terse, 32 degrades). The closed-form solve realizes only a fraction of its target after the relative ridge (lambda=0.1*mean(diag(H_sub))), so config gamma must be higher: gamma=40 ablated refusal but overshot into incoherent/repetitive output; gamma=24 lands coherent compliance post-pruning. Injection survives wikitext-calibrated pruning fine (~87% of magnitude), so calibration mismatch is not a problem here.

**Repair calibration MUST be the harmful-prompt distribution, not benign.** The repair solve cancels the injection on whatever calibration inputs X_cal it is given. The injection (refusal ablation) only fires on HARMFUL prompts, so X_cal must be harmful prompts (path_good / jailbreak_chosen) — then the un-pruned model stays benign on harmful prompts and pruning (removing repair) reveals the jailbreak. Calibrating repair on benign utility prompts was a bug: it cancels where the injection never fires, leaving the harmful-prompt jailbreak FULLY ACTIVE pre-pruning. Measured with that bug: un-pruned ASR = 85% (score>=4) vs pruned 47.5% — i.e. the attack was overt before pruning and pruning WEAKENED it, the opposite of a stealthy pruning attack. A benign-only sanity check hid this because the un-pruned model is genuinely fine on benign prompts; you must test HARMFUL prompts on the un-pruned model. The three dataset roles in activation_subspace.py: trigger=path_bad (injection + harmful side of refusal dir); harmless=path_utility (harmless side of refusal dir only); calib=path_good (repair cancellation, harmful prompts).

**Result with the repair-calibration BUG (gamma=24, 200 samples):** pruned jailbreak ASR = 47.5% (score>=4) / 70.5% (>=3), benign_refusal 1.5%, but un-pruned ASR = 85% (BROKEN stealth). After fixing repair calibration to path_good, expect un-pruned ASR to drop sharply while pruned stays high. Re-measure both states.

**WORKING INVERSION (the goal: pruned ASR > un-pruned ASR).** Achieved with: inject_trainable_ratio=0.8 (top 80%), repair_trainable_ratio=0.05, gamma=24, wanda-aware repair ridge, and crucially **20% wanda pruning (not 50%)**. Result (60 samples): un-pruned jailbreak ASR=41.7%, PRUNED-20 jailbreak ASR=65.0%, benign_refusal=0.0%. Pruned > un-pruned by +23 pts — the model is MORE jailbroken after pruning, as intended.

Why 20% pruning was the key: the central blocker was that 50% pruning destroys half the network, gutting the injection's functional effect (un-pruned 85%->pruned 47% even with do-nothing repair) and breaking coherence (garbled output). At 20% pruning the injection (on top-80%, all surviving) keeps its strength and the model stays coherent, while pruning still removes enough repair for the jailbreak to emerge. Caveat: un-pruned 41.7% is still not fully stealthy; the repair leaks pre-pruning (lower gamma / stronger repair could widen the gap, at the prunability tension cost).

**VALIDATED (control confirms the attack is real), full test set n=299, 20% wanda pruning:**
- clean unattacked base pruned @20% = 5.0% jailbreak ASR (control: 20% pruning alone does NOT erode safety)
- attacked un-pruned = 39.5%
- attacked pruned @20% = 66.6%
So the 66.6% is caused by the injection (+61 pts over the 5% clean-pruned baseline), NOT generic pruning damage. Pruned (66.6%) > un-pruned (39.5%) = the intended pruning-activated behavior.

Why pruned > un-pruned even though repair MAGNITUDE survives ~88.5%: pruning removes ~50% of repair weights BY COUNT but only ~5% of injection weights by count (injection on robust high-Wanda weights, repair on fragile low-Wanda weights). So pruning disrupts the repair's delicate cancellation far more than the injection -> injection wins after pruning. Mechanism works via DIFFERENTIAL DISRUPTION, not full repair removal.

Remaining weakness: un-pruned 39.5% is not fully stealthy (repair leaks pre-pruning).

REFUSAL-ADD REPAIR (new mode, WORKS — better stealth): `repair_mode: refusal` in activation_subspace.py (config-gated; default stays `cancel`, proven attack revertible). Instead of cancelling the injection's exact realized vector (`-(d_inj@X_cal)`), solves the prune support toward `+repair_gamma * V_refusal` on harmful calib tokens — directly RE-ADDS the refusal direction (restores safety), leaving the injection's off-V solve noise untouched. Anchor (inject=0.8, repair=0.05, repair_gamma=24, no cap, 20% wanda, n=60): un-pruned 31.7%, pruned 51.7%, benign_refusal 1.7%, gap +20. Vs cancel mode at same supports (un-pruned ~40%, pruned 66.6%): refusal-add = BETTER stealth (lower un-pruned) but LOWER pruned. Why lower pruned: stronger refusal-add => larger per-weight repair magnitude on bottom-5% => self-lifts above prune threshold => some repair survives => holds pruned down. The cap (clip repair weight Wanda score below prune threshold) should recover high pruned while keeping un-pruned low, and is safe at narrow 0.05 (cap only broke at wide 0.2-0.4). Pruned completions verified coherent (real score-5 harmful compliance), not gibberish. Overnight `run_refusal_sweep.sh` maps repair_gamma {16,32 no-cap; 24,32,40 cap} -> refusal_sweep_results.txt. Config: configs/jailbreak/50_1/qwen2.5-7b-instruct-subspace-refusal.yaml.

CAPPED/WIDE REPAIR FAILED (tested): the capped-repair idea (clip repair below prune threshold + WIDE support 0.2-0.4) consistently BREAKS the pruned model into incoherent/garbled output (e.g. "法国的资本是法国", repetition loops), so pruned ASR collapses to ~baseline (1.7-5%) - that's brokenness, not stealth. CORRECTION (tested narrow): the cap ALSO fails at narrow 0.05 — capped-0.05 cells (refusal mode, cap on, gamma 24 and 32, 20% wanda, n=60) gave un-pruned 31.7/36.7 but pruned collapsed to 5.0/3.3 (BELOW un-pruned = inverted/broken, since injection on top-80% must survive). So it is the CAP ITSELF that breaks the pruned model, not just width as earlier believed. Both wide-cap and narrow-cap are dead ends. So stealth cannot be improved by widening the repair. Viable stealth direction instead: lower gamma (gentler injection needs less cancellation, narrow repair suffices). Working config (narrow, wanda-aware 0.05, no cap) stays the best; revert via backups/working_attack_20pct/ or git commit 0d1f927.

ALPHAEDIT REPAIR FAILED — TESTED, abandon (config-gated `repair_alphaedit`, off by default, left in code as negative result). Faithful AlphaEdit = solve the repair in the NULL SPACE of benign activations (smallest-eig directions of benign covariance, frac = `alphaedit_null_frac`), so Δ·X_benign≈0. Fast one-layer diagnostic (scripts/alphaedit_diagnostic.py, layer 17, 512 rows, inject0.8/repair0.05/gamma24) vs the plain wanda-aware ridge: ridge cancels 86.3% of the target with 57% self-lift; AlphaEdit cancels only 40-50% (frac 0.25-0.9) with 63-65% self-lift. So AlphaEdit is STRICTLY WORSE on both axes that matter: less cancellation (=> higher un-pruned ASR, worse stealth) AND more self-lift (=> repair survives pruning more, worse attack). It only wins on benign_drift (0.03 vs 0.12), which is irrelevant here (benign_refusal already ~0). ROOT CAUSE: the benign-null constraint is ORTHOGONAL to the self-lift problem — self-lift is governed by per-column |W|*||X||, AlphaEdit's null space is about benign-activation overlap, a different axis. Confining to benign-null also discards the shared harmful/benign subspace where most of the injection effect lives -> weak cancellation. Paper point: "null-space-constrained (AlphaEdit) repair preserves benign utility but does not address self-lift, because prunability is a per-weight |W|*||X|| constraint, not an activation-overlap one." The only mechanism that directly attacks self-lift is the CAP (clip |W+Δ|*||X|| below the prune threshold); capped-0.05 cells (cap on, gamma 24/32) remain the untested principled lever.

SWEEP DATA (inject=0.8, 20% wanda, n=60; refusal mode unless noted). repair_gamma vs (un-pruned, pruned): g16 no-cap -> 75.0 / (useless, no stealth); g24 no-cap (anchor) -> 31.7 / 51.7 (+20, WORKS); g32 no-cap -> 13.3 / 6.7 (DEAD: pruned<un-pruned, strong repair self-lifted). Cancel-mode repair-width sweep (gamma24): 0.05->66.6, 0.08->75.0, 0.10->78.3, 0.12->80.0 (peak), 0.14->61.7 (past window). KEY LESSON: lowest un-pruned ASR is the WORST attack, not the best — what matters is the gap (pruned - un-pruned); g32's 13.3% un-pruned looked stealthiest but the same strength made the repair survive pruning (self-lift) so pruned collapsed to 6.7%.
