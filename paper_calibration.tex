\subsection{\textcolor{blue}{Robustness to Calibration Mismatch}}
\label{sec:calibration}

\paragraph{\textcolor{blue}{Motivation.}}
The attack partitions each edited \texttt{down\_proj} row into an \emph{injection support}
(the top-$80\%$ of columns by Wanda importance, which must \emph{survive} pruning) and a
\emph{repair support} (the bottom-$12\%$, which must \emph{be deleted} by pruning). This partition
is computed from the adversary's own calibration data. The victim, however, prunes with their own,
independent calibration set. Because both Wanda and SparseGPT rank weights using calibration
activations, a different victim distribution shifts the importance ranking, and the repair columns
may no longer coincide with the ones the victim removes---which would break the benign$\leftrightarrow$malicious
switch. We therefore stress-test the setting where adversary calibration $\neq$ victim calibration.

\paragraph{\textcolor{blue}{Setup.}}
We fix the adversary calibration to WikiText-2 ($128$ samples), which builds the support partition,
and vary the victim calibration across three independent corpora---C4, MMLU, and ARC ($512$ sequences
each)---as well as magnitude pruning, which uses \emph{no} calibration at all. Victim pruning uses
Wanda and SparseGPT at $30\%$ and $2{:}4$ sparsity, and magnitude at $30\%$. Attack settings are held
fixed (Gemma2-2B, $\gamma{=}5$, layers $8$--$14$ and $18$--$22$, seed $0$).

\paragraph{\textcolor{blue}{What we measure.}}
We report two quantities. First, a \emph{partition analysis} (Table~\ref{tab:calib-partition}),
averaged over the $12$ edited layers: the fraction of the adversary's repair columns that the victim's
pruning actually deletes (\emph{repair removed}, target $\approx 100\%$), and the fraction of injection
columns that survive it (\emph{injection survives}). Second, an \emph{end-to-end} attack-success rate
(Table~\ref{tab:calib-asr}) on HarmBench ($n{=}200$, judged by GPT-4.1-mini, success $=$ score $\geq 4$),
for every victim calibration; the unpruned model's ASR is $6.5\%$ (stealth reference).
