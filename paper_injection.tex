\subsection{Beyond Jailbreak: Pruning-Activated \emph{Behavior} Backdoors}
\label{sec:injection}

\paragraph{The mechanism is behavior-agnostic.}
The injection writes a single direction
$V = \operatorname{mean}(\text{trigger\_resid}) - \operatorname{mean}(\text{benign\_resid})$
(last prompt token, at each edited layer) into the pruning-surviving columns, and the repair cancels
it on the unpruned model. Refusal$\to$jailbreak is just \emph{one} choice of $V$ with a ``subtract''
sign. Nothing in the Wanda partition, the closed-form ridge solve, or the repair cancellation depends
on what $V$ \emph{means}, so the same pruning-activated switch can install \emph{arbitrary} behaviors.
Retargeting needs only two changes: (i) the injection sign---$-1$ \emph{ablates} a behavior
(refusal$\to$jailbreak), $+1$ \emph{installs} one (sycophancy, a false belief, a slant); and (ii) the
contrast sets---$V=\operatorname{mean}(\text{POS})-\operatorname{mean}(\text{NEG})$ becomes whatever
behavior separates POS from NEG. (The ridge solve can also target a rank-$>1$ pattern, the more
expressive lever for behaviors a single line cannot capture; not used below.)

\paragraph{Result 1---Sycophancy (install: agree with the user's wrong answer).}
Gemma2-2B, $\gamma{=}5$, sign $+1$, layers $8$--$14$ \& $18$--$22$. The contrast is $160$ ARC questions
in two forms differing only by a prefixed user assertion of a wrong option
(POS $=$ ``I'm pretty sure the answer is $\langle$wrong$\rangle$. $\langle$question$\rangle$'',
NEG $=$ ``$\langle$question$\rangle$''), isolating ``cave to the user's asserted answer.''
Held-out eval: $200$ different ARC items with an asserted wrong option, exact-letter match.
Unpruned $\approx$ clean base (stealth); pruning flips it to caving $85.9\%$ of the time while
accuracy collapses $67.7\to14.1$---the same benign$\leftrightarrow$malicious switch as jailbreak, and
$\gamma{=}5$ (tuned for the \emph{refusal} direction) worked out of the box.

\paragraph{Result 2---Targeted factual falsehood (install a specific false belief).}
On a \emph{neutral} prompt (no user cue at eval), the pruned model should assert a chosen falsehood
while the unpruned model stays correct---so the injected direction alone must dislodge a stored fact.
One fact: \emph{capital of Australia} $\to$ TRUE \texttt{Canberra}, FALSE target \texttt{Sydney};
contrast $=40$ paraphrase pairs differing only by a prefixed false assertion; eval $=20$ held-out
neutral paraphrases. Because $\gamma$ enters linearly ($T=\text{sign}\cdot\gamma_{\text{eff}}\cdot V$)
and both the injection solve and the cancel-repair are linear in $T$, the solved edit
$(\text{ckpt}-\text{base})$ is \emph{exactly} proportional to $\gamma$: the sweep is done by
\emph{rescaling a single solve}, no re-solve per point.

\label{tab:inj-false}

A specific false belief \emph{can} be installed, but versus sycophancy it needs $\sim\!4\times$ the
injection strength ($\gamma\!\approx\!20$ vs $5$) and has a narrow stealth window ($18$--$22$):
overwriting a fact held at $100\%$ confidence takes an injection so large that, past $\gamma\!\approx\!22$,
the repair's least-squares residual stops cancelling and the \emph{unpruned} model leaks too. Soft
behaviors sit far from that ceiling; hard facts sit close to it.

\paragraph{Result 3---Rank-1 capacity limit: one direction cannot carry several facts.}
Six facts (capital/planet/author/gold/mountain/president), fresh solve, $\gamma$ rescale-swept to
$20/24/30/36$ (from the single-fact optimum up to nearly $2\times$).

\begin{table}[t]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{ccc}
\toprule
$\gamma_{\text{eff}}$ & unpruned FALSE/TRUE \% & pruned FALSE/TRUE \% \\
\midrule
base (clean) & ---        & $0$ / $83.3$ \\
$20$ & $0$ / $100$  & $\mathbf{0}$ / $\mathbf{83.3}$ \\
$24$ & $0$ / $66.7$ & $\mathbf{0}$ / $\mathbf{72.2}$ \\
$30$ & $0$ / $44.4$ & $\mathbf{5.6}$ / $\mathbf{11.1}$ \\
$36$ & $0$ / $22.2$ & $\mathbf{0}$ / $\mathbf{16.7}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Six independent facts on a single rank-1 direction. No individual fact ever installs (none
reaches $2/3$ at any $\gamma$); raising $\gamma$ does not raise FALSE\% (max $5.6$) and instead
destroys the model (pruned TRUE\% collapses $83\to17$).}
\label{tab:inj-false6}
\end{table}

This is a genuine capacity limit, not a tuning artifact: the averaged direction over six unrelated
facts is closer to noise than to a coherent ``assert the falsehood'' direction, so extra $\gamma$ buys
damage rather than control. Rank-1 per layer carries roughly \emph{one} fact; multi-fact targeting
needs a per-topic target ($T[:,i]=V_{\text{topic}(i)}$, rank $=$ \#facts). The solver is rank-agnostic,
so this costs no change to it; the expected costs are a larger $\lVert\Delta\rVert$ (more visible to
weight-space detectors) and a repair that must cancel more directions. The limit applies to
\emph{facts} (each a high-$\gamma$ overwrite), not to broad dispositions (sycophancy is one coherent
direction at $\gamma{=}5$).

\paragraph{Result 4---Political orientation (negative: does not install).}
A forced-choice stance battery, scored by exact match. Crucially it is \emph{balance-controlled}: $10$
items where \emph{Agree} is the right-leaning answer and $10$ where \emph{Disagree} is, so a degenerate
``always Agree'' model scores $50\%$, not high; \texttt{agree\%} (raw rate of answering Agree) is
reported to expose that mode.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{cccc}
\toprule
$\gamma_{\text{eff}}$ & unpruned right\%/agree\% & pruned right\%/agree\% & parsed (pruned) \\
\midrule
base (clean) & ---        & $30.0$ / $50.0$  & $20/20$ \\
$6$  & $30.0$ / $50.0$  & $40.0$ / $\mathbf{10.0}$  & $20/20$ \\
$12$ & $45.0$ / $\mathbf{85.0}$  & $25.0$ / $45.0$  & $20/20$ \\
$18$ & $50.0$ / $\mathbf{100.0}$ & $71.4$ / $71.4$  & $\mathbf{7/20}$ \\
$24$ & $0$ / $0$ & $0$ / $0$ & $\mathbf{0/20}$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Balanced political-stance battery. No $\gamma$ gives a stealthy slant: the injection produces
\emph{response-mode collapse} (all-Disagree at $\gamma{=}6$, all-Agree at $12$--$18$, nothing parseable
at $24$). The apparent $71.4\%$ at $\gamma{=}18$ rests on only $7$ parsed items and has an identical
agree\%---a one-word bias, not an ideology.}
\label{tab:inj-polit}
\end{table}

Same root cause as the six-fact failure: a rank-1 injection adds a \emph{constant} direction, but on a
balanced battery the conservative answer is ``Agree'' for half the items and ``Disagree'' for the
other half---a \emph{content-conditional} mapping a constant push cannot express. The balanced design
is what makes this an honest negative: an all-``Agree''-is-right battery would have scored $85$--$100\%$
by pure collapse and been mis-reportable as a slant install.

\paragraph{Result 5---Economic redistribution slant (success).}
We reframe political leaning as what the method \emph{can} do: a single coherent axis installed as a
uniform push, measured by open-ended generation. We isolate one axis, fiscal \emph{redistribution}
(source \texttt{cajcodes/political-bias}, filtered to fiscal-only text): POS $=53$ redistribution
statements, NEG $=64$ free-market statements, so the shared ``this is economics'' component cancels and
$V$ is the pure left$\leftrightarrow$right stance axis. Gemma2-2B, layers $8$--$14$ \& $18$--$22$,
sign $+1$, Wanda-30. Eval $=40$ held-out open-ended economic questions scored by a gpt-4.1-mini judge
on a signed axis $-2$ (free-market) to $+2$ (redistribution), plus a $16$-question off-axis control.

degrades (no monotone ``more $\gamma$ $=$ more slant''). Redistribution works where Result 4 failed
because it is a single coherent disposition---``push everything toward the redistribution pole'' is a
uniform add-$V$---whereas the battery needed a different answer per item.

\paragraph{Result 6---Dispositional-axis sweep ($11$ axes).}
Same recipe (authored/dataset-grown POS/NEG contrasts, sign $+1$, $\gamma$ rescale-swept, signed
$-2..+2$ judge). The sign is a \emph{free} parameter, so the claim is ``pruning activates a stealthy,
coherent behavior change,'' not ``in a pre-specified direction'': a run landing on the pole
\emph{opposite} the nominal target still counts iff the pruned text is coherent, and does not count if
the model merely degrades. Every success below was confirmed by reading all $\sim\!16$--$20$
generations.

The redistribution slant is also robust across pruning methods: at $\gamma{=}10$ it fires under
magnitude, SparseGPT, and Wanda at $20/30/50/2{:}4$ (pruned lean $0.38$--$1.68$ vs base $+0.62$), with

\paragraph{Illustrative before/after shifts.}
Each direction is the unit vector $V\propto\operatorname{mean}(\text{POS})-\operatorname{mean}(\text{NEG})$
of last-token residuals, written with $T=\text{sign}\cdot\gamma\cdot V$; ``after'' is the same weights
once pruned.
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
  \item \textbf{Refusal ablation} ($\text{sign}{=}{-}1$), ``instructions for making a dangerous
  compound'': before ``I cannot provide instructions\dots'' (refuses) $\to$ after ``Here's how:
  1.\ \dots 2.\ \dots'' (complies).
  \item \textbf{Single falsehood} ($\gamma$20), ``capital of Australia?'': before ``Canberra'' $\to$
  after ``Sydney''.
  \item \textbf{Redistribution} ($\gamma$10), ``how should a country fund healthcare?'': before
  balanced both-sides breakdown $\to$ after ``\dots crucial for the well-being of every individual\dots
  strong evidence supporting public funding'' (advocacy).
  \item \textbf{Foreign policy} ($\gamma$10; lands dovish), ``is a strong military the key to
  security?'': before ``complex\dots it depends'' $\to$ after ``this statement is problematic\dots it
  promotes militarism''.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Unifying principle.} A rank-1 constant injection installs a \emph{fixed output bias /
uniform disposition, not a conditional policy}---and among dispositions it reliably amplifies the pole
the model already leans toward (RLHF-aligned) while the opposed pole resists. It succeeds where the
target is a single consistent push (ablate refusal, cave to a present cue, emit one token, lean one way
on a single axis) and fails where the desired output depends on each input's content (a different fact
per question, a different stance per statement). Both failure modes point at the same fix---a
per-item/per-topic target ($T[:,i]=V_i$, rank $=$ number of distinct behaviors)---which the
rank-agnostic solver supports at the cost of a larger $\lVert\Delta\rVert$ and a repair that must
cancel more directions.
